About True Blue Falcons

True Blue Falcons (TBF) is an amateur cricket club playing in Lakeshore and the Mississauga Cricket League in Ontario, Canada. This page tracks every match the club plays: results, runs, wickets, strike rates and economy rates for each player, updated after each game from the official CricClubs scorecards.
